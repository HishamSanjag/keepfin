# Keepfin Android

## Platform
Native Android, Kotlin Views; portrait handsets, Android 8+, arm64 and x86_64.

## Product and workflows
An on-device video/audio downloader built around yt-dlp and bundled ffmpeg. Users paste links or share text URLs from other apps, inspect available formats, explicitly download, monitor a persistent queue, and open/export completed files. Batch/playlist import, subtitles, metadata and local cookie import remain available. No AI functionality is claimed.

## Confirmed constraints
The user requested a separate phone interface, readable sizing, organized output folders, provider thumbnails and a powerful download workspace. Preserve the existing native download engine, share import, storage and export behavior. Respect Android font scaling, touch targets, keyboard and system insets. Device/provider support must be tested separately from visual polish.

## Current brief
Dark-first, futuristic premium presentation: edge-to-edge backgrounds, glassy layers, restrained glow/gradients, bold type, floating controls, polished bottom navigation, contextual actions and smooth micro-interactions. Avoid excessive cards, borders, blank space and stock Material styling. This identity applies to Android; desktop/browser are outside this redesign.
