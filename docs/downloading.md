# Downloading

Keepfin works with YouTube, YouTube Music, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Reddit, Vimeo, Twitch, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Pinterest and more than 1,000 other sites.

## How a download works

1. **Read.** Keepfin opens the link and finds out what it is: a video, a song, a playlist, a channel or a photo post.
2. **Choose.** You pick the quality or audio format. Single videos and songs show their size first.
3. **Download.** Several downloads run at once. After network trouble they retry and continue where they stopped.
4. **Save.** The file gets its title, artist, cover and details, and goes into your folder.

## Video

Pick any quality the site offers, up to 4K and 8K, including HDR and 60 fps, saved as MP4, MKV or WebM.

Many sites send picture and sound separately. Keepfin joins them into one file without re-encoding, so the quality stays the same as the original.

**Extras** such as subtitles, cutting a part of a video and chapters wait on their own tab in the preview.

## Audio

Pick MP3 (320 kbps by default), M4A, Opus, FLAC or WAV. Songs are saved with their title, artist, album, cover and lyrics.

A note on quality: audio on video sites is already compressed. M4A and Opus usually keep that sound exactly as it is, MP3 is converted for older players, and FLAC can't add back quality that wasn't there.

## YouTube Music and YouTube

YouTube Music links open on Audio and are saved as songs. YouTube links open on Video. You can keep both a song and its music video: when you switch between them in the player, they stay on the same second.

## Spotify, Apple Music and Deezer

Paste a track, album or playlist link, check the matches, then tap **Download**.

These services lock their audio, so Keepfin never takes audio from them and needs no account there. It reads the song's public details and finds the same recording on YouTube Music, comparing title, artist and length. It prefers official songs, and avoids live, remix, sped-up and karaoke versions unless the original is one.

If nothing matches closely enough, Keepfin tells you instead of saving the wrong song.

## Playlists, albums and channels

1. Paste the link. Items load in pages of 200, so even huge playlists open quickly.
2. Untick what you don't want, or use **Select all**.
3. Tap **Download**. Each item appears in Downloads as it finishes.

Songs you already have are unticked and labelled. Tick one again, or use **Download anyway**, to save it twice.

**Keep in sync:** turn this on when you download a playlist or channel, and Keepfin checks for new items as often as you choose. You can also tap **Check now** on the collection at any time.

## Photo posts (Windows)

Links to posts with photos on Instagram, X, Pinterest, Reddit, Tumblr, Flickr and others save every image in the post into one folder.

## Private, age-restricted or members-only videos

Keepfin can use your own sign-in, so a site shows it what your account can already see.

- **When a link needs it:** the preview says the site shows the post only to signed-in people. Choose **Sign in to** that site, sign in in the window that opens, and Keepfin reads the link again by itself. **Not now** keeps the message.
- **Android:** open **Settings › Downloads › Sign in to a site** and sign in inside Keepfin.
- **Windows:** open **Settings › Downloads › Website sign-in › Sign in to a site…** and sign in in the window that opens. To undo it, choose **Sign out** beside the site. Keepfin never sees your password and keeps the sign-in encrypted on your computer. You can also use the browser you're signed in with, or import a cookies.txt file. Google does not allow signing in from Keepfin's window, so for YouTube sign in in Firefox and Keepfin uses that sign-in automatically.

Downloads start signed out. Only when a site says it needs a sign-in does Keepfin try yours, once, and the download's row then says "Signed in" (or "Signed in with Firefox" on Windows). To keep every download signed out, turn off **Settings › Downloads › Website sign-in › Use a sign-in when a site asks for one**. On Windows, a cookie source you choose yourself is always used.

Your sign-in is used only for downloads and never leaves your device.

## Managing downloads

- **Choose where one download goes:** tap **Save to** next to Download.
- **Change the defaults:** Library › Edit › Where downloads go.
- **Download on another device:** choose **Download on…** and pick a connected device.
- **Failed items:** open the playlist group and use **Retry failed** or **Remove failed**, or the menu on one item.
- **Run more or fewer at once:** Settings › Downloads › Parallel downloads.

Removing something from your download history doesn't delete the file. Deleting a file is a separate step that always asks first.
