# Your folder, copies and devices

## The Keepfin folder

Everything you download lives in **one folder you choose**. The files inside are normal files: your music plays in any other player, and your videos open in any other app. Keepfin keeps its own library data in a hidden `.keepfin` folder alongside them.

You pick the folder during first start. You can move it later in **Settings › Storage & copies › Change location…** (on your phone, under **Folder location**). Keepfin shows you the plan first and checks every file in the new place before it switches. On Windows you can keep the old place as a backup copy; on your phone the old folder stays until you choose to delete it, and Keepfin never deletes it by itself. Spaces that live outside your Keepfin folder show a notice with **Bring them in**.

## Copies

A **copy** keeps your folder safe somewhere else. Set them up in **Settings › Storage & copies**.

**Where to:** another folder, an SD card or USB drive, a cloud account (Google Drive, Dropbox or WebDAV) or another of your devices.

**How it works:**

| Kind | What it does | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Backup** | Keeps earlier versions and what you delete | Keeping everything safe |
| **Mirror** | An exact copy; files removed from it wait 30 days in its trash | A plain copy of your folder |
| **Two-way** | Changes on either side reach the other | Two places you use equally |

Cloud accounts connect directly to your own account. There's no Keepfin server in between.

## Your devices

Connect your phone and PC once, and they can work together.

**Connect them:**

1. Open Keepfin on both, on the same Wi-Fi.
2. On one of them, open **Settings › Devices** and press **Connect**.
3. Check that both show the same six-digit code, then tap **Codes match** on both.

Nothing is shared until you allow it, device by device.

**Then you can:**

- Play and copy each other's files
- **Download on another device:** paste a link as usual, choose **Download on…** and pick the device
- **Combine libraries:** open the other device in Settings › Devices and follow the steps
- Stay connected away from home: add a device by its address or Tailscale name in **Settings › Devices**

To rename a device, open **Settings › Devices** and tap its name.
