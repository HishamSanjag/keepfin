# Library and player

The library keeps everything you've saved organized and ready to play. If you'd rather have a plain downloader, switch to **Just downloads** in **Settings › Library & playback › Keepfin mode**.

## Spaces

A **space** is a home for one kind of thing, such as Music or Videos. Open **Library** and tap a space to open it.

- **Create, color, pin or reorder:** on Windows press **Edit Library** at the top of Library; on the phone, Library › Edit. Drag a card to move it, tap the pin to show or hide it on the home page, and tap **+ New space** to add one.
- **Smart spaces** fill themselves: choose **Smart**, add rules, and watch the live count.
- **Show one kind at a time:** tap a chip such as Videos. Tap All to see everything again.

## Home: mixes and new releases

- **Made from your listening:** Home builds mixes from your own plays and library: On repeat, Forgotten favourites, Recently added music, Never played, Because you play an artist you play a lot, and a Late-night mix (songs you play after 10 pm). They are made on your device and never leave it. A mix appears once it has five songs. Tap a mix to open it, or **See all**.
- **Follow an artist:** in Library, open **Artists** and press **+ Follow** on any row, or press **Follow** on the artist's page, or open a song's menu and choose the **Follow** entry with the artist's name.
- **New releases card:** Keepfin looks about every 12 hours for new releases on YouTube Music. While there are releases you have not seen, Home shows one small card with a few covers and **Review**. Review lists them under **This week** and **Earlier this month**; nothing downloads on its own, press **Keep it** to save a release with your usual settings. The **x** hides the current releases (the card comes back only for newer ones). **Don't show on Home** removes the card for good; turn it back on in Settings, Library, **Show new releases on Home**.
- **Who you follow:** in Library, Artists, choose **Following** to see everyone you follow, when they were last checked, and **Check now**. An artist that could not be checked shows a quiet note and is tried again later.

## Arrange Home and Library

- **Sections:** tap the pencil at the top of Home or Library (**Edit Home**, **Edit Library**). Each section gets an eye to hide or show it and **Row** or **Grid**. On Windows, drag a section's handle (or focus it and press the up and down arrow keys); on the phone, use the section's **⋮** menu, **Move up** or **Move down**. Tap **Done**; **Reset** puts the page back as it came.
- **Card size and text:** while editing, the bar at the bottom has **Card size** (80% to 125%) and **Text** under cards: title and info, title only, or none. On Windows they are also in **Settings › Appearance › Cards**, and **Ctrl +** and **Ctrl −** change the size anywhere (**Ctrl 0** resets, or hold Ctrl and turn the mouse wheel).
- **A wider window shows more cards.** They grow only a little, never all at once.

## The sidebar (Windows)

- **Edit sidebar:** press the pencil at the bottom of the sidebar. Drag the handles to reorder Home, Search, Library and Downloads, press the eye to hide a row, and the pin to keep a space or playlist at the top under **Pinned**. Right-click any row for **Pin to top** or **Hide**.
- **Floating or docked:** the **Floating** switch while editing, or **Settings › Appearance › Sidebar**.
- **Width:** drag the sidebar's right edge; double-click it to go back. **Ctrl+B** collapses it to icons.

## Collections

Collections are your playlists, plus the albums and playlists you've downloaded.

- Open a space, then the **Collections** tab, or open Library › Collections › See all.
- Tap **+** to make your own playlist.
- Tap **▶** to play one collection, or **Play all** to play the whole space.
- To move or copy a collection to another space, open its menu **⋮** and choose **Move to space…** or **Copy to space…**.

## Adding to your library

Open a space and tap **+ Add**, then:

- **From a link** downloads straight into that space.
- **Device files** copies files you already have into it.

## Finding and fixing

- **Search** looks across your library.
- **Tags:** open a space and tap a tag chip, or **Tags ▾** for all of them. Choose Any or All, then tap Play or **Save as smart space**. To add your own, select items and tap **Tags…**.
- **Favorites:** tap **♡** in the player, or open an item's menu **⋮**. They're in Library › Favorites.
- **Fix a title, artist or cover:** open the item's menu **⋮** and choose **Edit info**.
- **Lyrics:** open Lyrics in the full player. On the phone, a card under the controls shows the line being sung: tap **✕** on it to hide it, and **⋮ › Show lyrics preview** to bring it back. To turn lyrics off, go to Settings › Downloads › Add lyrics to songs.

## Select, undo and trash

- **Select:** long-press an item or tap **Select**, then choose Move, Tags…, Add to playlist or Delete.
- **Undo:** tap **Undo** in the notice before it closes.
- **Trash:** deleted files wait in **Settings › Storage & copies › Trash** for 30 days. Tap **Restore**, or **Delete for good…** to erase one. To skip the trash, tick **Delete for good** when you delete.

## Music player

1. Tap any song.
2. Tap the bar at the bottom to open the full player.
3. Swipe the cover left or right for the next or previous song.

Music keeps playing in the background, and you can use your lock screen and media keys. Tap **✕** on the bar to stop.

**Equalizer and bass boost:** open the full player's menu **⋮** and choose **Equalizer**.

**Crossfade (Windows):** Settings › Library & playback › Crossfade.

## Video player

Tap a video in your library, then tap the screen for controls. The player's menu **⋮** has chapters, speed and **Take screenshot**. Keepfin remembers where you stopped, under Continue watching.

On Windows, press your mouse's back button or Alt+Left to go back a page.
