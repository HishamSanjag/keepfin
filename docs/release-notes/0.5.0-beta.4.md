# Keepfin v0.5.0-beta.4

The fourth test version of the next big update. Install it over beta 3; your library, history, settings and files carry over. Betas come through **Settings › Updates › Update channel › Beta**, and switching back to Stable never removes anything.

## New

- **Activity.** A bell at the top shows what Keepfin did for you: new songs from playlists you keep in sync, finished playlists, downloads that failed, updates and sign-ins that need attention. When you come back after a while, a short notice tells you how many things happened.
- **Lyrics for your songs.** Keepfin looks up lyrics on LRCLIB, a free lyrics library, when a song finishes downloading and saves them with the song. Timed lyrics follow along in the player. It's on by default: **Settings › Downloads › Add lyrics to songs**.
- **Keep in sync, when you choose it.** Downloaded playlists and channels no longer follow their link on their own. Turn on **Keep in sync** right on the download screen, pick how often, or switch it on later in Follow settings. Playlists you already follow keep their setting.
- **Check now tells you what it found.** "Found 3 new songs in Gym Mix", or "No new songs", appears in the app. The collection page shows the new songs downloading.
- **Download straight into a space.** In any space, **+ Add › From a link** opens the download screen with that space already chosen.

## A player that feels alive

- The mini player grows into the full player as one surface. Drag it up to open and down to close.
- Play and pause change shape smoothly, and the song that's playing shows moving bars in the list.
- The seek bar grows when you press it and shows the time under your finger. On Windows, the volume slider stretches when you drag past the end.
- Lyrics glide so the current line stays in the middle.
- Everything is instant when your system's reduce-motion setting is on.

## Android

- The mini player has a Previous button.

## Fixed

- Settings and layout checks are steadier on slower computers.
