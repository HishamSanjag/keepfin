# Keepfin v1.1.0

Photo posts, sign-in that happens by itself, faster and safer copies, Home mixes and following artists, a sidebar and pages you arrange yourself, and the phone catching up with Windows.

## Windows

- **Photo posts.** Keepfin saves the photos of a post, one photo or a whole carousel, from Instagram, Pinterest, Reddit, X, Tumblr and more. The preview shows the photos in a grid; tap a photo to leave it out, and the button says how many will be saved.
- **Sign-in when a site asks.** Downloads go signed out. When a site needs a sign-in, Keepfin uses one it can find (a Firefox sign-in on this PC, or Keepfin's own) and tries again. The download's row says "Signed in with Firefox". Turn it off in Settings › Downloads › Website sign-in.
- **Sign in inside Keepfin.** When no sign-in is found, the preview offers "Sign in to Instagram", which opens the site's own login page in a separate Keepfin window. Keepfin never sees your password, and the sign-in is kept encrypted on this PC. Settings › Downloads › Website sign-in lists your sign-ins with Sign out.
- **Faster copies.** Copies to Google Drive need about half the requests, upload four files at a time, and no longer download unchanged files again. Adding a cloud account goes straight to the copy's setup.
- **Keepfin's own data stays small.** Its records are packed into a few files instead of thousands, so copies and starting Keepfin are quicker. Existing folders are packed automatically after the update.
- **Clearer Storage.** Counts show your files, not Keepfin's own data, and removing a copy shows its progress and says when it is done. Dropbox and Google Drive copies show the service's own mark.
- **Dropbox copies sync again.** Keepfin now sends Dropbox one change at a time, as Dropbox asks, instead of stopping with "could not be reached". The first sync after the update tidies old record files and can take a few minutes.
- **Photos open fitted to the window**, with zoom on + or the mouse wheel and 0 to fit again.
- **Photo posts everywhere.** "Download on…" sends the chosen photos to another device, "Save again" saves only the photos that were missing, and a post already saved is not downloaded twice.
- **Home: mixes and new releases.** Home builds mixes from your own plays and library (On repeat, Forgotten favourites, a Late-night mix and more). They are made on your PC and never leave it. Follow an artist with one click in Library › Artists, on their page or from a song's menu: Keepfin looks for new releases about every 12 hours, and a small card on Home says when something is new (Review shows them; turn the card off if you like). Nothing downloads until you press Keep it. With music videos turned on in Settings, songs you keep get their music video too, and "Download on…" sends a song's music video along when you chose it.
- **The sidebar is yours to arrange.** Floating or docked, drag its edge to change the width, and Edit sidebar to reorder or hide pages and pin spaces and playlists to the top.
- **Edit Home and Edit Library.** Show, hide and reorder sections, and show each as a row or a grid. Card size and the text under cards are in the same bar, in Settings › Appearance › Cards, and on Ctrl + and Ctrl −.
- **Cards look the same everywhere**, and a wider window shows more of them instead of making them bigger. Home is what to play next; Library is everything you keep, by kind.
- **Pair with a typed code.** Settings › Devices can pair with a 10-character code typed from the other device, for when the QR code cannot be scanned.
- **"Play from" names the right device.** An item that is not on this PC says which paired device actually holds it.
- **Safer copies.** A renamed or moved file reaches your copies as a move instead of a new file. Copies also check for names that differ only in how their letters are encoded, for files that are only online in a cloud app, and for files too big for a FAT-formatted drive, and say so instead of failing halfway. Smart spaces can be picked when you choose what a copy keeps.
- **Sizes for every video.** When a site lists no file sizes (Instagram, for one), Keepfin asks the site for each file's real size. Where a site offers only one or two sizes, Keepfin also offers smaller ones it makes after downloading, marked "converted". It never makes a video bigger than the original. An item's details now show its resolution, frame rate, codecs and bitrate.
- **Updating looks like an update.** Running the installer over an installed Keepfin says "Update Keepfin" and updates it where it is, without asking for a folder again.
- **Security updates** to the app's components.

## Android

- **Photo posts**, with the same photo grid as on Windows. Photos go to Pictures/Keepfin, or your space's folder.
- **Sign-in when a site asks.** Downloads go signed out; when a site needs a sign-in, Keepfin uses your Keepfin sign-in for that site and tries again. The row says "Signed in". Turn it off in Settings › Downloads › Website sign-in.
- **"Sign in to Instagram" in the preview** opens that site's login page straight away, and the link is checked again after you sign in.
- **Keepfin's own data stays small**, the same as on Windows, and removing a copy shows its progress.
- **Smoother tab switching** from a startup profile built into the app.
- **Move the Keepfin folder.** Settings › Storage & copies › Folder location › Change location… moves the whole library to another folder or memory card, checking every file in the new place before it switches. Spaces kept outside the folder can be brought in with Bring them in.
- **Storage like on Windows.** Remove a copy, Free up space, and the same choices when you add a copy.
- **Home: mixes and new releases**, the same as on Windows. Follow an artist from their page or a song's actions; kept songs get their music video when you keep music videos, and "Download on…" sends it along when you chose it.
- **Full-player styles** Stage, Cinema and Afterglow, and the full player now follows your Light, Dark or System theme.
- **Music videos as on Windows**: a Song | Video switch on songs that have a music video.
- **Edit Home and Edit Library**, the same as on Windows: show, hide and move sections, row or grid, plus Card size and the text under cards. Cards look the same on every screen, and Jump back in fits two tiles per row.
- **Pair with a typed code** and **"Play from" names the device** that holds the item, the same as on Windows.
- **Safer copies**, the same checks as on Windows, including moves for renamed files.
- **"Meet your library"** shows after an update's What's new instead of being skipped.
- **Sizes for every video**, the same as on Windows: real sizes where a site lists none, and smaller "converted" sizes where a site offers only one or two. An item's Info shows its resolution, codecs and bitrate.
- **Dropbox copies sync again** and cloud copies show the Dropbox or Google Drive mark, the same as on Windows.
- **The Instagram sign-in page shows its login form** instead of a blank page.
- **An update you started can be found again.** If you close the update while it downloads, Settings › About Keepfin shows its progress, and Show update brings back the Install button.

## Update notes

- Install over 1.0.2 or any earlier version, on Windows and Android. Your library, history, settings and files carry over.
- After the first sync with this version, a Google Drive copy moves its old small record files to Google Drive's trash once; Google Drive empties its trash after 30 days.
