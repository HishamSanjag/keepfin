# Keepfin v0.5.0-beta.3

The third test version of the next big update. Install it over beta 2; your library, history, settings and files carry over. Betas come through **Settings › Updates › Update channel › Beta**, and switching back to Stable never removes anything.

## New

- **Features & how-to.** Every feature with the steps to use it, searchable, with "Try it" to jump there. Find it in Settings, on Library home and in What's new.
- **Move or copy a playlist or album to another space** from its menu, or with **+ Add** in the space you want it in.
- **Swipe the music player (Android).** Swipe the cover or the mini player left or right for the next or previous song. Swipe the full player down to shrink it, and the mini player down to close it.
- **The lock screen and media card open the player (Android).** Tapping the media card now opens Keepfin on the song that is playing.
- **Every library list has its options.** All collections gets New collection, sorting and a filter (playlists, albums, channels, yours). All spaces, albums, artists and "See all" lists get Play, Shuffle and Select.
- **Remove failed songs from a playlist download.** Each failed or waiting song has Remove, and the playlist has **Remove failed**. Saved files are never touched, and a followed playlist won't download a removed song again.

## Faster playlists

- YouTube and YouTube Music playlists now download as many songs at once as your **Parallel downloads** setting allows. If YouTube starts limiting your network, Keepfin drops to two at a time for 30 minutes, then goes back to your setting.
- On Android, Parallel downloads now starts at 3 instead of 2. If you chose a number yourself, it stays.

## Quieter notices

- One "Saved" notice when a batch finishes instead of one per song. A new notice replaces the old one, and every notice has a close button. On Android you can also swipe it away.
- The mini player has a close button: it stops the music and hides the bar.

## Fixed

- On Windows, saving the library could rarely fail when two saves overlapped.
