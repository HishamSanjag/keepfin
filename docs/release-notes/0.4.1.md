# Keepfin v0.4.1

Playlists now stay together, failed downloads retry on their own, and music gets proper album covers.

## Both platforms

- Playlists, albums and channels can be kept together: one entry in Downloads and Library with the source link, overall progress such as "8 of 12 done", and actions for the whole set. Choose **Separate items** before downloading to keep the old behaviour.
- Downloads that fail for temporary reasons retry automatically up to three times, and the row shows the countdown. A download waiting to retry never holds up the others. If the download engine looks like the cause, Keepfin updates it before retrying.
- When YouTube limits downloads from your network, Keepfin says so, waits longer between retries and slows down the playlist instead of failing song after song.
- Error messages are more accurate: "removed" only appears when the site says the media was removed, and failed downloads have **Error details** with a copy button.
- YouTube Music links default to audio, with a setting to prefer video.
- Music gets square album covers. Songs from Spotify, Apple Music and Deezer links get the original album art as well as the original title, artist and album.
- One notification per playlist when it finishes, for example "11 of 12 saved, 1 failed", and downloads that finish together share one notification.
- Opening a download from a notification jumps to it once and then leaves scrolling to you.
- Settings has one **Updates** page: Keepfin itself, and the download engine, which updates automatically.

## Windows

- The download engine's version no longer sits in the sidebar; it only appears when it needs attention.

## Android

- Downloads keep running at full speed in the background and with the screen off, and the progress notification appears as soon as a download starts.
- New empty screens for Downloads, Library and searches with no results.
- Notifications show the Keepfin icon.

## Update notes

- Playlist artwork now also appears for personal YouTube Music playlists that don't include it.
- Your history, settings and files carry over from 0.4.0.
