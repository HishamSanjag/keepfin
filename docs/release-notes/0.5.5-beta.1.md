# Keepfin v0.5.5-beta.1

The first test version of 0.5.5: a new look built around downloading, more exact music matching, and one shared engine that makes Windows and Android decide everything the same way. Install it over 0.5.0; your library, history, settings and files carry over. Betas come through **Settings › Updates › Update channel › Beta**, and switching back to Stable never removes anything.

## A new look, with downloading first

- **Home** opens with one big box: paste a link to download it, or type to search. Below it, **All · Videos · Music · Photos** filters what you see, then your recent items, Continue watching, rows made from your listening, what's downloading now, and new videos and photos. Rows you have nothing for stay hidden.
- **Search** finds songs, videos, albums, artists, playlists and posts in your library first, and offers to search YouTube for anything else.
- **Choose how you use Keepfin.** New installs ask once: **Downloader only** (just paste, pick the quality and find the file, no library or player) or **Downloader + library & player**. Change it any time in **Settings › General › Keepfin mode**. Your current setting is kept.
- **Album, artist and playlist pages** have a large cover with a colour band taken from the artwork, Play and Shuffle, and the total time and size.
- **One menu everywhere.** Right-click or ⋯ on Windows, and long-press or ⋯ on Android, show the same actions for an item wherever you are. Actions that don't apply say why.

## Windows

- One sidebar: Home, Search, Library and Downloads (with a live count), then your spaces, playlists, albums and artists. It folds to icons on narrow windows. Settings and Activity sit at the top.
- The player bar has a favourite heart, the file format, a Lyrics button and a **Now playing** panel with what's up next. Lyrics open full width.
- **Speed, sleep timer and output** fit in one short panel.
- **Scroll the mouse wheel over the volume** to change it, in the music player and the video player.
- **Fixed:** dragging a song in the queue (or a playlist) moves it instead of showing "Drop to inspect".

## Android

- A bottom bar with Home, Search, Library and Downloads (with a count), and a **+ Download** button on Home and Library.
- When you open Keepfin with a link copied, it offers once to download it. Turn this off in Settings if you prefer.
- The full player takes its colours from the artwork and shows the current lyric line under the controls. **Song info** shows where a song came from.
- A **Pure black** option for the dark theme, and Material You colours are labelled as such.
- Subtitles you download are saved as SRT files, which more players understand.
- Quality labels read like Windows ("1080p · MP4", "MP3 · 320 kbps").

## Music

- **Albums match exactly.** A Spotify, Apple Music or Deezer album is matched track by track to the same album on YouTube Music, and the release year picks the right edition. A single track link finds its album too.
- Other versions of a song with the same length, such as a Drumless Edition, a cappella or demo, are no longer picked instead of the original.

## Downloads

- **Long titles are short now.** Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and other posts that use their whole caption as a title get its first line, without view counts, links or hashtags, cut at a word.
- When you open a playlist or album, **every song is selected** so you can download it straight away. Channels and endless mixes still start empty.
- After you download from a playlist or album, its review closes instead of staying on screen.
- The expected size of an audio download shows before it starts.

## Both apps decide the same way

- Windows and Android now share one engine for reading links, choosing formats and sizes, building downloads, reading progress, explaining errors, spotting songs you already have, sorting into spaces and checking versions. Each decision is tested against the same cases on both apps.
- Songs you already have are recognised the same way on both apps. An artist such as "Feather" is no longer split as a "feat." credit.
- Smart spaces match album names regardless of accents.

## Update notes

- This is a beta: please report anything that looks wrong. It installs over 0.5.0 on both Windows and Android.
