# Keepfin v1.0.0-beta.1

The first test version of Keepfin 1.0. It keeps everything you save in one place you own, the Keepfin folder, and lets your PC and your phone share it: copies on a drive or in the cloud, devices that sync and play from each other, and downloads you can send from one device to another. Install it over 0.5.5; the first start moves your existing library into the new folder and keeps everything. This is a beta: please report anything that does not work as described.

## Both apps

- **Your Keepfin folder.** Your music, videos, photos, playlists and history live in one folder you choose. The first start asks whether you are new or bringing a Keepfin folder, and moves an existing library across safely. Library details are kept in a hidden `.keepfin` folder inside it.
- **Copies.** Keep a copy of your library on another folder, an SD card, a USB drive, a paired device or a cloud account. Choose what to keep and how: a backup that keeps earlier versions, a two-way copy that brings changes back, or a mirror. Needs review, Restore and Clean up help when something needs your decision. Folder copies can also export playlists, a flat folder and MP3 versions.
- **Cloud accounts.** Google Drive, Dropbox and WebDAV (OneDrive is planned for a later version). Keepfin keeps its copy in its own folder in your account and never asks for more than it needs.
- **Devices on your network.** Pair your PC and your phone once with a six-digit check (or a QR code). Paired devices sync your library, play songs and videos from each other without saving them first, keep a copy of your library, and can send a download to each other with "Download on…". Nothing is shared until you allow it, device by device.
- **Away from home.** Add a device by its address or Tailscale name; Keepfin keeps trying in the background and shows when it was last reachable.
- **Finding devices.** Devices find each other even when your router blocks device discovery: while Devices is open, Keepfin also asks the other devices on your network directly, and shows them under Nearby with a Pair button.
- **Tailscale.** With Tailscale on both devices, Keepfin finds the other device through it (shown as "via Tailscale"), pairs with it, and reconnects paired devices wherever they are. Keepfin keeps the addresses a device tells it, so a device that moved is found again.
- **Settings, reorganised.** Nine categories with the same names and order on both apps: Downloads, Library & playback, Storage & copies, Devices, Appearance, Notifications, Help, Support Keepfin and About Keepfin. Search finds any setting, and a short "Needs your attention" list at the top points to anything that needs fixing.
- **Setup you can see.** The first start and the move of an existing library show what a Keepfin folder holds, where your files go and how much space they need, step by step.
- **Devices that explain themselves.** "How devices work" on the Devices page says what finds what, what pairing does, what syncs by itself and what stays on each device. Every paired device shows when the library last synced with it.
- **Pairing that guides you.** The device you start pairing from waits until the other one is open to pairing (Devices, then Pair or Add by address or code), and a pairing request shows up wherever you are in the app.
- **A calmer, more compact look.** Every page is built the same way: the same bar at the top, the title in the same place, one row of controls. One place to search, and the accent colour only on things you can press.
- **Undo for everything in the library.** Moving, renaming, tags, favorites, playlists, removing a space, deleting a file and clearing download history all show a notice with Undo, which reverses only that change. Notices close by themselves.
- **Trash.** Files you delete from the Keepfin folder wait in Trash for 30 days. The Trash page shows how long each one has left, and brings files back with their tags, favorite, plays and playlist places, even after a restart. Delete for good skips the trash; Undo still works until its notice closes.
- **Favorites** is a space of its own, gathering what you added to your favorites from every space.
- **One kind at a time.** Spaces that hold videos, audio and photos together show a chip for each kind.
- **Name your devices.** Rename a device in Settings → Devices, and your other devices learn the new name at once. Combine libraries now says clearly when the other device hasn't shared its library yet, and what to do on it.
- **Help and Support** are now separate, and the guide in Help covers everything new.
- **Playlists.** Your own lists are called playlists on both apps; Collections still gathers playlists, albums and channels. Clear upcoming empties the queue after the current song and keeps it playing.
- **A new logo.** Keepfin's new logo is the Ink Tide shark rising from a wave, on the app icon, the installer, About and the first start. Settings → Appearance → App icon still lets you choose the earlier icons instead.
- **A start screen with the logo.** Both apps open on the Keepfin logo and name instead of a blurry icon or an empty window.
- **Songs and their videos.** YouTube Music links are audio by default and YouTube links are video; you can keep both. A song and its video stay on the same second when you switch. The video has its own row, plays on its own and can be deleted while the song stays. Downloading the video of a song you already have adds it to that song.
- **Lyrics.** New songs get their timed lyrics again: Keepfin asks again when the lyrics service is busy, prefers timed lyrics over plain words, and says so when a song only has plain words. Find lyrics looks again for a song that has none.
- **A clearer download preview.** A pasted link opens one window (a full-height sheet on the phone) with Video, Audio or Both, every size in one list with its format always visible, and Extras (subtitles, cut, chapters) on a tab of its own. Keep in sync stays on the main page for playlists you follow. Add to playlist puts the download straight into one of your playlists.
- **One kind of row in Downloads.** A video in a playlist looks like any other download, with its picture, length, details, Play and menu. A playlist is one row that opens with its arrow; it starts closed once everything is saved.
- **The right pictures.** Saved videos show their own cover instead of a scene from the video, also in playlists.
- **Same choices on both apps.** MP3 starts at 320 kbps for a song, a playlist or several links. WebM is offered only for videos that can be saved as WebM. The size shown before downloading is the size the download shows afterwards.
- Continue watching says "0:11 left" when less than a minute is left.

## Windows

- Settings → Storage & copies shows your Keepfin folder and its copies; Settings → Devices pairs and manages devices.
- Windows Firewall: an install for all users allows Keepfin on every network, including the Public one Windows gives a new network. If Windows still blocks other devices (a per-user install, or a dismissed prompt), Devices shows "Allow Keepfin", which fixes it with one Windows prompt.
- File names in Arabic, other scripts and with emoji are kept exactly. Downloads saved under a damaged name by earlier versions are found again and open normally.
- After the move into the Keepfin folder, Downloads opens and shows every finished download in its new place (the history used to keep pointing at the old folders).
- Downloads is more compact: one short line under the link field, and an open album or playlist shows a small cover with its songs below.
- The player: the line being sung stands out, the lyrics header stays in place, and going to another page closes the lyrics. The whole bar at the bottom opens the player and has the same ⋯ menu as a song. Scrolling down shrinks the full player again.
- Buttons on cards sit on their own background, readable on any artwork. The collapsed sidebar opens from the logo, smoothly.
- Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+K or / to search, Ctrl+, for Settings, Space to play or pause, Esc to close. The list is in About & help.
- The theme follows Windows unless you choose one in Settings → Appearance.
- The installer is sharp on high-resolution screens.
- Songs and videos that are on another device are listed with "On <device>" and play from it.
- The app icon setting changes the window, the taskbar and Keepfin's Start menu and desktop shortcuts.
- The player has an equalizer with ten presets and bass boost, like the phone, and video subtitles have a size.
- The mouse back and forward buttons (and Alt+Left/Alt+Right) go back to where a page was scrolled, and pages with a path at the top have a back arrow. The sidebar has a Support button next to the version.
- Updates install by themselves: Restart and update closes Keepfin, updates it without any installer pages and opens it again.
- A Windows MSI is available for IT and managed installs; it is updated by installing the next MSI.
- In the Downloads grid, a playlist opens in a window over the grid with all its items, Retry failed and the folder.
- A video frame you save goes to your downloads folder; Copy image in its notice puts it on the clipboard. While a video plays, notices show at the top so they never cover its controls, and an open menu is always above notices.

## Android

- Links open faster: the download engine keeps what it learned about YouTube between links.
- Sharing a link to Keepfin no longer closes the app on some Android 15 phones.
- Scrolling back to the top of a list quickly no longer sends the rows off the screen.
- Downloads → All lists every saved download, newest first, instead of three and a "See all" button.
- The Download button stays on screen when a video's preview opens at half height.
- The Keepfin folder is now part of setup on the phone too, as on Windows. If you skipped it before, Keepfin asks once more and brings your library across.
- First start: one button, "Create my Keepfin folder", makes your Keepfin folder (Documents → Keepfin) and opens Android's confirmation right inside it; tap "Use this folder" and "Allow", that is all. "Other options" lets you choose another place or an existing Keepfin folder.
- Files an earlier Keepfin saved (also after a reinstall) are found and offered at the start, and Settings → Storage can bring them in later. Your download history follows every file that moves.
- Moving into the Keepfin folder continues by itself if the app was closed in the middle.
- Every tab has the same two buttons at the top (activity and settings), and search lives in the Search tab.
- Settings → Storage → Copies keeps copies on an SD card, a USB drive, another folder, a paired device or a cloud account. Large scheduled copies wait until the phone is charging or on Wi-Fi.
- Settings → Devices pairs with your PC or another phone. "Reachable when Keepfin is closed" keeps it available to your other devices in the background. Files wait for Wi-Fi unless you allow mobile data for a device.
- A pairing request from another device shows as a dialog, or as a notification when Keepfin is in the background.
- Songs and videos that are not on the phone play from a paired device that has them.
- The video player has chapters, Take screenshot (saved to Pictures/Keepfin) and Previous and Next; the full player has Go to artist.
- Menus that slide up from the bottom no longer bounce.
- The app icon setting changes the icon on your home screen. After this update your home screen may lose the Keepfin icon once; Keepfin stays in your app list, where you can add it back.
