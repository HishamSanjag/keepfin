# Keepfin v0.4.0

Keepfin: a new download engine and a redesigned interface on Windows and Android.

## Both platforms

- New self-updating download engine, kept current automatically, with a stable or nightly channel and a manual update button in Settings.
- A real format picker lists resolution, frame rate, HDR and codec, with an estimated size that includes the audio track.
- Audio downloads as MP3, M4A, Opus, FLAC or WAV, with cover art and tags.
- Parallel downloads, with pause and resume.
- Playlists, channels, albums and mixes load progressively in pages, with selection and **Load more**. YouTube Music playlists no longer load forever.
- Spotify, Apple Music and Deezer links are matched to recordings on YouTube Music and tagged with the original metadata. No audio comes from those services, and a match may not always be the exact recording.
- Private and age-restricted content works through sign-in or cookies.
- Subtitles, SponsorBlock, chapters and trim options.
- Friendlier error messages with suggested fixes.
- An app-update indicator in the header, and **What's new** after updating.
- Redesigned interface.
- A Support page, if you would like to back Keepfin's development.

## Windows

- YouTube JavaScript challenges are solved with the app's built-in runtime, so full format lists are available.
- Image posts and galleries download through the built-in gallery engine.
- Use cookies from Firefox or other browsers. Firefox is the most reliable; Chromium-based browsers can lock or encrypt their cookies on Windows.
- Proxy and speed-limit settings.
- Optional Turbo transfer, off by default.
- Drag and drop links, or paste with Ctrl+V, to capture them.
- A verified engine is bundled in the installer, so the first run works without downloading tools.

## Android

- Rebuilt with Jetpack Compose and Material 3, including dynamic color.
- Smoother progress: download rows update individually, with no list refreshes or thumbnail flicker.
- In-app sign-in for YouTube, Instagram, X, Facebook, TikTok and more.
- Sizes are shown on every quality.
- Optional multi-connection transfers, off by default.
- A Wi-Fi only option.

## Update notes

- Provider behavior, sign-in requirements and speeds can still vary by site and change over time. The built-in engine updater helps keep downloads working.
