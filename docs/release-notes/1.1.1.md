# Keepfin v1.1.1

The music already on your phone or PC, in Keepfin, and public Instagram photo posts without a sign-in.

## Windows

- **Music on this PC.** Library has a new card: press Show my music and the songs already on this PC appear in a "This device" space, where they are. Nothing is copied or moved. Keepfin looks in your Music folder; add or remove folders in Settings › Library & playback › Music on this PC. New songs show up by themselves, and ringtones, recordings and clips under 30 seconds are left out.
- **These songs stay on this PC.** They, and your favorites, plays and playlist entries for them, are not saved in your Keepfin folder and do not go to your other devices. Remove from Keepfin hides a song; Delete file sends it to the Recycle Bin after you confirm.
- **Public Instagram photo posts save without a sign-in.** Keepfin reads the post as a signed-out visitor sees it and saves its photos straight away; it signs in only when the post needs it.

## Android

- **Music on this phone.** Library has a new card: press Show my music, allow access to your music, and the songs on your phone appear in a "This device" space, where they are. Nothing is copied or moved. New songs show up by themselves; ringtones, notification sounds, recordings and clips under 30 seconds are left out, and Settings › Library & playback › Music on this phone can leave out more folders. The songs are also in Android Auto.
- **These songs stay on this phone.** They, and your favorites, plays and playlist entries for them, do not go to your other devices. Remove from Keepfin hides a song; Delete file asks Android first.
- **Public Instagram photo posts save without a sign-in**, the same as on Windows.
