# Keepfin v0.4.2

Songs that YouTube blocks in your region now download anyway.

## Both platforms

- Some YouTube Music songs, often "Topic" tracks, are blocked by YouTube for listeners without an account in certain regions, even though they play in the YouTube Music app. Keepfin now recognises this and saves the same song from another upload of it, with the original title, artist and album cover. The download shows "Saved from another upload of this song".
- Pasting a link to one of these songs works too: the review shows the original song and downloads it from another upload.
- When no other copy exists, the download fails at once with a clear explanation and the option to sign in to YouTube, instead of retrying three times with "Retrying may help".

## Update notes

- Your history, settings and files carry over from 0.4.1.
