# Keepfin v1.0.1

Google Drive and Dropbox copies work in this version. Keepfin 1.0.0 showed both as "Not available in this build".

## Both apps

- **Google Drive and Dropbox are available.** In Settings › Storage & copies you can now add a copy on Google Drive or Dropbox: sign in, and Keepfin keeps a copy of your library there. OneDrive is planned for a later version.

## Windows

- **Instagram photo posts explain what they need.** Instagram now shows photo posts only to people who are signed in. Keepfin says so ("Sign-in required") instead of "No downloadable formats"; use your browser's sign-in in Settings › Downloads and try the link again.
- Pressing Esc twice quickly while the player opens or closes no longer leaves an error behind.

## Update notes

- Install over 1.0.0 or any earlier version, on Windows and Android. Your library, history, settings and files carry over.
