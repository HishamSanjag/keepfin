# Keepfin v0.5.0

Keepfin is now a library and a player too. Everything you save gets a home, playlists can stay in sync on their own, and you can watch and listen without leaving the app.

## Your library

- **Spaces** keep your media organised: Music, Videos and Downloads to start, plus any space you create, such as "Watch later". Each space has its own folder, subfolders, file names, color and icon.
- **Save to** sits next to every Download button. Links from YouTube Music, Spotify, Apple Music and Deezer go to Music, and everything else goes to Downloads. In any space, **+ Add › From a link** downloads straight into it.
- **Collections.** Every playlist, album or channel you download stays together in its original order. Play one collection or the whole space, and move or copy it to another space. Your own playlists live here too.
- **Tags and smart spaces.** Tags come from the source site, and you can add your own. A smart space gathers items from everywhere by rules (tags, collections, source, artist, kind or date added) without moving any files.
- **Library home** with Continue watching, Recently added, Recently played and Favorites. Browse music by songs, albums, artists and playlists; videos show as posters with progress bars; photos open full screen. Search, sort, filter, grid or list, and select several items to move or delete.
- **Songs you already have are not downloaded twice.** Playlists mark them "Already in Music" before you download.
- **Add files** brings songs and videos you already have into a space. The library only shows what Keepfin saved or what you add, never your whole drive or phone.
- **Edit info** fixes a title, artist, album or cover. Rename and move files between spaces safely.
- Turn the library off in Settings › Library to go back to the plain downloader. Nothing on disk changes.

## Keep playlists in sync

- Turn on **Keep in sync** when you download a playlist or channel, and Keepfin checks it on a schedule you pick and downloads only the new items. Songs you deleted or removed are never downloaded again, and big batches wait for you to review them.
- **Check now** tells you what it found ("Found 3 new songs in Gym Mix"), and the collection shows the new songs downloading.

## A player that feels alive

- **Music:** a player bar on every tab that grows into the full player as one surface, a queue you can reorder, shuffle and repeat, favorites and playlists, a sleep timer, playback speed, even volume between songs, and media keys and lock-screen controls.
- **Lyrics** are looked up on LRCLIB, a free lyrics library, when a song finishes downloading and saved with it. Timed lyrics glide along in the player. On by default: Settings › Downloads › Add lyrics to songs.
- **Video:** resume where you left off, chapters and subtitles, speed and audio track choice, picture-in-picture and fullscreen, A-B loop and Up next.
- Play and pause change shape smoothly, the playing song shows moving bars, and the seek bar grows under your finger with the time. Everything is instant when your system's reduce-motion setting is on.

## Activity and help

- **Activity.** A bell at the top shows what Keepfin did for you: new songs from synced playlists, finished playlists, failed downloads, updates and sign-ins that need attention. Opening an entry marks it read. Come back after a few days and a short notice tells you what happened.
- **Features & how-to.** Every feature with the steps to use it, searchable, with "Try it" to jump there.
- **Updates are easy to spot:** a short popup when a new version is out (Update now, Later or Skip) and an **Update channel** choice of Stable or Beta in Settings › Updates.

## Faster, calmer downloads

- Playlists download as many songs at once as your **Parallel downloads** setting allows. If YouTube starts limiting your network, Keepfin drops to two at a time for 30 minutes, then goes back. On Android the setting now starts at 3.
- Failed songs in a playlist download each have **Remove**, and the playlist has **Retry failed** and **Remove failed**. Saved files are never touched.
- One "Saved" notice when a batch finishes instead of one per song, and finished downloads stay under the Finished filter in Downloads.

## Windows

- Video player keyboard shortcuts, picture adjustments, aspect and crop modes, frame screenshots and a choice of audio output device.
- Crossfade between songs, up to 12 seconds.
- MKV videos that Windows can play are prepared once, losslessly, and then play in Keepfin.
- Drag files onto a space to add them, drag items out to other apps, and export playlists as M3U8.

## Android

- Music keeps playing in the background with notification, lock screen, headset and Android Auto controls, and resumes after a restart. Tapping the media card opens the player.
- Swipe the cover or mini player for the next or previous song, as many times in a row as you like, with shuffle on or off. Swiping right goes straight to the previous song, and the start or end of the queue tells you so. Swipe the player down to shrink or close it. The mini player has Previous and close buttons.
- Equalizer with presets and bass boost, and video gestures: double-tap to seek, swipe for brightness and volume.
- Settings are grouped into sections, each with its own page.
- "Find my earlier Keepfin files" brings back files saved before a reinstall.

## Update notes

- Your history, settings and files carry over from 0.4.2. Your current download folder becomes the Downloads space.
- Coming from beta 4: swiping between songs no longer stops working after a couple of swipes (especially with shuffle on), and Activity entries lose their dot as soon as you open them.
