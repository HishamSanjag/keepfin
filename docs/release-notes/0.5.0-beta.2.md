# Keepfin v0.5.0-beta.2

This is the second test version of the next big update. Install it over beta 1; your library, history, settings and files carry over. If you want betas, choose **Settings › Updates › Update channel › Beta**. If you switch back to Stable, nothing is removed: you move to the next stable version when it's released.

## New: Spaces that organise themselves

- **Collections.** Every playlist, album or channel you download stays together as a collection, in its original order. Play one collection or the whole space. Your own playlists live here too, marked "Yours".
- **Follow a playlist or channel.** Keepfin checks it every 6 hours (on Wi-Fi, on Android) and downloads only the new items. Songs you deleted or skipped are never downloaded again. More than 20 new items wait for you to review them. You can choose "Check now" only, or switch following off.
- **Tags.** Tags come from the source site, and you can add your own. Filter a space by one or more tags and play the result.
- **Smart spaces.** A space can gather items from everywhere by rules: tags, collections, source, artist, kind or date added. No files move.
- **Colors and icons** for every space.
- **A tidier Library home.** It shows your pinned spaces as compact cards, with "All spaces" for the rest. Edit spaces right in the same grid: drag to reorder (built-in spaces too), pin, and add a new space.
- **Add from your library.** "Add" on a space now offers your songs, videos and collections first, to move or copy them in. Files from your device are still an option.

## Also new

- **Update channel:** Stable or Beta, in Settings › Updates.
- **Open Keepfin on:** Downloads, Library or "Where I left off".
- **Settings pages** show a clear way back.

## Fixed

- On Android, the Library tab could close the app after you deleted every song. Fixed.
- Deleting many items now shows progress and a result.
- Downloaded playlists now appear in the Library as collections.
- Downloads history shows when saved files were deleted ("Deleted", or "2 of 5 files removed").
- On Android, songs with featured artists ("feat.") now match the right YouTube Music recording.
- On Android, size estimates no longer count some audio twice.
- Radio mixes opened from a song load again.
- The Windows and Android apps now make the same choices for formats, errors and links. A shared set of checks keeps them identical.
