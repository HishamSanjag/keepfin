# Keepfin v0.5.5-beta.2

The second test version of 0.5.5, with fixes from the first round of testing. Install it over beta 1; your library, history, settings and files carry over.

## Windows

- **The Library page shows everything:** your spaces, playlists and collections, with a filter row (All · Spaces · Playlists · Collections · Albums · Artists · Videos). The separate Collections view is gone, and every page you open has a way back to the Library.
- **Finished downloads play in Keepfin.** A finished song or video has a Play button, and clicking its picture plays it too. "Open in another app" is in the ⋯ menu. In Downloader only mode, Open still uses your default app.
- **Grid view shows albums and playlists as cards**, the same size as single downloads, with the cover, the number of items and the album or playlist label.
- **Collapse the sidebar** to icons with the button next to the logo, or Ctrl+B. Keepfin remembers your choice.
- **Resize the Now playing and queue panel** by dragging its edge. Double-click the edge to reset it.
- **The Windows media panel shows the real cover** of the song or video that's playing instead of a black square.
- The "Your library" filters sit on one tidy row, the selected space shows its icon again, and the bottom of the sidebar has clear "Support Keepfin" and theme buttons.
- Clicking the Keepfin logo opens Home.
- Lyrics have their own icon instead of a microphone.

## Android

- **Swiping between songs keeps working** when you swipe quickly several times in a row. A new swipe no longer picks up movement from the one before it.
- **Finished downloads play in Keepfin** when you tap them. "Open in another app" is in the menu.
- **Playlist, album and artist pages** blend the artwork colour into the top bar, which turns solid with the title as you scroll. Play and Shuffle always show their full names.
- **Rows on Home and Library keep the same height** when they mix videos and music, so nothing jumps while you scroll sideways.

## Both apps

- The Downloads page lists the sites Keepfin works with: YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Reddit, Vimeo, Twitch, Pinterest and 1,000+ more.
