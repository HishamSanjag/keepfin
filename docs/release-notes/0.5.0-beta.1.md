# Keepfin v0.5.0-beta.1

This is a test version of the next big update. Install it over your current app; your history, settings and files carry over. When the final 0.5.0 is out, Keepfin offers it as a normal update.

Keepfin is now a library and a player too. Everything you save gets a home, and you can watch and listen without leaving the app.

## Both platforms

- **Spaces** keep your media organised: Music, Videos and Downloads to start, plus any space you create, such as "Watch later". Each space has its own folder, subfolders and file names.
- **Save to** sits next to every Download button. Links from YouTube Music, Spotify, Apple Music and Deezer go to Music, and everything else goes to Downloads. A random audio grab never lands in your music. Change the defaults in Settings › Library.
- **Songs you already have are not downloaded twice.** Playlists mark them "Already in Music" before you download, and single links offer "Download anyway".
- **A new Library tab**: Home with Continue watching, Recently added, Recently played and Favorites. Music is browsable by songs, albums, artists and playlists, videos show as posters with progress bars, and photos open in a full-screen viewer. Search, sort, grid or list views, and select several items to move or delete.
- **Built-in music player**:
  - a player bar that keeps playing on every tab
  - a queue you can reorder, shuffle and repeat
  - favorites and your own playlists
  - synced lyrics
  - a sleep timer and playback speed
  - even volume between songs
  - media keys and lock-screen controls
- **Built-in video player**:
  - resume where you left off
  - chapters and subtitles
  - speed and audio track choice
  - picture-in-picture and fullscreen
  - A-B loop
  - Up next
- **Add files** brings songs and videos you already have into a space. The library only shows what Keepfin saved or what you add, never your whole drive or phone.
- **Edit info** fixes a title, artist, album or cover. Rename and move files between spaces safely.
- **Downloads** now holds finished downloads too, under the Finished filter. Library is only for your files.
- Turn the whole library off in Settings › Library to go back to the plain downloader. Nothing on disk changes.

- **Updates are easy to spot**: a short popup when a new version is out (Update now, Later, or Skip this version) and a banner you can hide.
- **Collections with a failed item** show up as soon as nothing is downloading, with "9 saved · 1 failed", **Retry failed**, and the failed items listed first.
- **Select all** wherever you can select several items.
- Smaller items by default so more fits on screen, with a Comfortable option per space.

## Windows

- Keyboard shortcuts in the video player, plus picture adjustments, aspect and crop modes, frame screenshots, and a choice of audio output device.
- Crossfade between songs, up to 12 seconds.
- MKV videos that Windows can play are prepared once, losslessly, and then play in Keepfin.
- Drag files onto a space to add them, and drag items out to other apps.
- Export playlists as M3U8.

## Android

- A new Library home: your spaces as cards at the top (tap one to open it, Edit to rename or reorder, long-press for more), with your shelves below.
- Settings are now grouped into sections, each with its own page.
- The link box takes one line.

- Music keeps playing in the background with notification, lock screen, headset and Android Auto controls. After a restart, playback resumes where you stopped.
- Equalizer with presets and bass boost.
- Video gestures: double-tap to seek, swipe for brightness and volume.
- "Find my earlier Keepfin files" brings back files saved before a reinstall.

## Update notes

- Your history, settings and files carry over. Your current download folder becomes the Downloads space.
