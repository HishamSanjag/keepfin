# Questions and answers

## About Keepfin

**Is it really free?**
Yes. Keepfin is free and open source under the [MIT License](../LICENSE). If it's useful to you, you can [support its development](https://github.com/sponsors/HishamSanjag).

**Do I need an account?**
No. There's no Keepfin account and no Keepfin server.

**Is there an iPhone or Mac version?**
Not at the moment. Keepfin is for Windows and Android.

**Are the two apps the same?**
Almost. A few things exist on one app only: photo posts, crossfade and moving your folder from Settings are on Windows, and sharing a link straight to Keepfin is on Android.

## Privacy

**What does Keepfin connect to?**
Only the sites you download from, YouTube Music to match songs, LRCLIB for lyrics, and its own update feed. If you set up cloud copies, it also connects to your cloud account.

**Does it track me?**
No. There's no analytics or tracking.

**Is my sign-in safe?**
Your sign-in is used only for downloads and never leaves your device. Keepfin can't open anything your account can't.

**What happens if Keepfin crashes?**
It offers you the details to copy the next time it opens. They stay on your device unless you send them yourself.

## Files

**Where are my files?**
In your Keepfin folder. They're normal files you can open with any app.

**If I uninstall Keepfin, do I lose my files?**
No. Your folder stays where it is.

**Does clearing my download history delete files?**
No. History and files are separate, and deleting a file always asks first.

**I deleted something by mistake.**
Tap **Undo** right away, or restore it from **Settings › Storage & copies › Trash** within 30 days.

## Updates

**How do I update?**
Keepfin Setup on Windows installs updates quietly, and the Android app updates from inside the app. For the Portable EXE or the MSI, download the new version from the [downloads page](https://github.com/HishamSanjag/keepfin/releases/latest).

**How do I get new features sooner?**
Choose Beta under Updates in **Settings › About Keepfin**.

**Why does a download stop working on one site?**
Sites often change how they serve media. Keepfin's download engine checks for updates once a day, so most problems fix themselves within a day or two.

## When something goes wrong

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| A video says it's private or age-restricted | [Sign in](downloading.md#private-age-restricted-or-members-only-videos), then try again |
| A download failed | Open it on Downloads and tap **Retry**. Keepfin continues where it stopped |
| Downloads got slower | If YouTube limits your connection, Keepfin runs fewer at once for a while, then goes back to your setting |
| A song didn't match | Keepfin won't save a wrong match. Try a link to the song on YouTube Music |
| Something else | [Open an issue](https://github.com/HishamSanjag/keepfin/issues) with your app, its version (Settings › About Keepfin) and the link |
