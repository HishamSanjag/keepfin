# Keepfin domain terms

**Download choice**: the picks for one download (Video, Audio or Both; the quality, container, audio format and audio language). A screen keeps one in its own state and asks `app.keepfin.core.choice.DownloadChoice` about it. Avoid: "selection", "format settings".

**Offer**: what the preview shows to pick from for a link (its qualities, containers and audio formats, each allowed or not, with the reason). Avoid: "options", "available formats".

**Opening choice**: the picks a preview starts with, from the default quality, the kind of link and the music-videos setting. Avoid: "default choice", "initial selection".
